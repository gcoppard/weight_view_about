Privacy Policy for WeightView – Weight Loss Tracker

Effective date: November 11, 2025

WeightView (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the WeightView app.

Information We Collect

App Usage & Diagnostics

  • Firebase Analytics: Used to understand how users interact with the app and improve features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect anonymized crash and error data to improve stability.

User Feedback

  • If you choose to submit feedback through the app, we collect your rating and any text you voluntarily provide.
  • Feedback text is optional and should not include personal or sensitive information.
  • Feedback is stored securely using Firebase Firestore.

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data only to:

  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Identify and fix bugs or crashes
  • Understand feature usage
  • Enhance the overall user experience

Third-Party Services

WeightView uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Firestore

These services are provided by Google and may collect anonymized device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google’s privacy policies.

Learn more about how Google handles data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Retention

Analytics data, crash reports, and user-submitted feedback are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill their purpose or until deletion is requested.

Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data protection laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

You may have the right to:

  • Request access to data associated with your app usage
  • Request deletion of submitted feedback or analytics data (where applicable)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Contact us regarding how your data is used

To make a request, please contact us at weightviewapp@gmail.com.

We rely on legitimate interest to process limited analytics and feedback data for app improvement. Some data may be processed on servers operated by Google and handled in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell or rent user data. All collected data is handled securely and processed in accordance with Firebase and Google security standards.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
